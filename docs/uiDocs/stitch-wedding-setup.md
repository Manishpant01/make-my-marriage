# Stitch prompt — start a wedding

Follow [typography.md](typography.md) for the approved Outfit + Fraunces font system; it overrides older font-family instructions in this prompt.

For application animations, follow [animations.md](animations.md): title, photograph, and form entrances with stable name/date fields and role selection on desktop and mobile.

Paste this into Stitch as a new design. Match the landing page and the log in screens that already exist: same logo, same ivory, same type, same bronze. This is a new layout. Do not reuse the log in split.

## Prompt

Design the start-a-wedding screen for MakeMyMarriage. The person is already signed in. This is the first thing they fill in for a new wedding: the bride’s name, the groom’s name, the first day, the last day, and whether they are the bride or the groom. English only.

Match what already exists. Same wordmark. Same warm ivory background. Same near-black text. Same bronze. Same modern grotesque. Same slightly rounded controls, about 8px. Not a pill. Not a sharp square. Calm, premium, and quiet.

Make two frames:

1. Desktop, 1440 wide
2. Mobile, 390 wide

Color, nothing else:

- Background: warm ivory #F6F3EE
- Text: near-black #1C1917
- Secondary text: warm gray #6B6560
- Buttons: muted bronze #8C6A45, label #FFFCF8
- Input borders and hairlines: #E7E1D8
- Input fill: #FFFCF8

This is not the log in layout. Do not split the frame into a photograph on one side and the form on the other. Do not put the photograph to the left of the form, or to the right of the form. The page is one centered column. The photograph is a wide, low band inside that column, between the title and the fields.

Header, full width, same as the signed-in home. A hairline under it. Logo on the left: the wordmark “MakeMyMarriage” in the modern grotesque, near-black, with the small bronze open circle to its left. On the right, only “Priya · Log out”. Nothing else in the header.

Desktop. Under the header, one column about 680 wide, centered, with generous space above it. Top to bottom inside that column:

- A small line in warm gray: “Your wedding”
- Title: “Start the wedding”. Clear, but smaller than the landing headline.
- One sentence in warm gray: “The couple’s names, and the first and last day.”
- One photograph, the full width of the column, about 240 tall. Corners about 8px. No border, no caption, no overlay, no text on the image.
- A hairline, the full width of the column.
- The form, in two columns with a comfortable gap.
- Then the choice, full width.
- Then the button.

The photograph is new. Do not reuse the landing photograph or the log in photograph. One image only. A wide, low crop, seen from above: a cream invitation on linen, in natural warm daylight, with a single small flower. No people, no hands, no faces, no stage, no crowd, no red-and-gold decor.

Form, two columns. Labels sit above the fields, in the near-black. Fields are empty. No sample text inside them. No calendar popup.

- Left: label “Bride’s name”, then the field. Right: label “Groom’s name”, then the field.
- Left: label “Start date”, then the field. Right: label “End date”, then the field.

Under the fields, one label: “I am”. Then two equal options on one row. Each option is text in a quiet box: hairline border, fill #FFFCF8, corners about 8px. “The bride” is selected: the border is bronze, the words are near-black. “The groom” is not selected: the border is #E7E1D8, the words are warm gray. No icons. No radio dots. No solid bronze fill.

Under the choice, a bronze button, left aligned, not the full width of the column: “Start the wedding”.

Mobile, 390 wide. The same header: logo left, “Priya · Log out” right. No photograph in the header. The column uses the width of the screen with comfortable side margins. The same order: the gray line, the title, the sentence, the photograph, the hairline, then the form. The photograph is shorter, about 160 tall, still a wide band, still the new image. The four fields stack in one column, in this order: Bride’s name, Groom’s name, Start date, End date. “I am” stays one row of two options. The button is the full width of the column. Text does not overflow. Touch targets are easy to tap.

Do not add a sidebar, a second image, icons, an illustration, a fake browser, or a phone frame. Do not add events, guests, tasks, a budget, an invitation message, or a family invite. Do not add prices, testimonials, or extra sentences. Do not change the colors.
