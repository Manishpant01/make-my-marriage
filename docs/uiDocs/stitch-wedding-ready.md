## Prompt

Design the screen a person sees just after they save a new wedding on MakeMyMarriage. The wedding now exists. This page congratulates them. It shows the couple’s names, the dates, one short message, a little art, and one button. English only.

Match what already exists. Same wordmark. Same warm ivory background. Same near-black text. Same bronze. Same modern grotesque. Buttons use a small corner radius, about 8px. Not a pill. Not a sharp square.

The feeling is a printed congratulations card, handed to the couple. Premium, quiet, and a little ceremonial. Not a party poster. Not a festival banner.

Make two frames:

1. Desktop, 1440 wide
2. Mobile, 390 wide

Color, nothing else:

- Background: warm ivory #F6F3EE
- Text: near-black #1C1917
- Secondary text: warm gray #6B6560
- Button: muted bronze #8C6A45, label #FFFCF8
- Card and hairlines: #E7E1D8
- Card fill: #FFFCF8
- The drawing: bronze #8C6A45

Header, full width. A hairline under it. Logo on the left: the wordmark “MakeMyMarriage” in the modern grotesque, near-black, with the small bronze open circle to its left. On the right, only “Priya · Log out”. Nothing else in the header.

Desktop. The page below the header is empty ivory, with the card placed in the vertical and horizontal center. One card only, about 480 wide. Fill #FFFCF8. Corners about 8px. A hairline border, and a second hairline inset by about 12px, like a formal invitation. No shadow.

Inside the card, everything is centered, with generous padding, about 56px. Top to bottom:

- One small drawing, centered. A single marigold stem in fine bronze line, with a few small leaves. The flower head is a soft wash between ivory and bronze, not a bright orange cartoon. It sits in empty paper. It should feel like the small ornament on an expensive printed wedding card.
- A small line in warm gray, with a little space between the letters: “Congratulations”
- The names, large, near-black, medium weight: “Priya and Rahul”
- A short bronze rule, about 48px wide and 1px tall, centered under the names
- The dates, in warm gray: “12–14 March 2027”
- One message, near-black, two short lines: “The wedding is ready.” then “The days are set.”
- A bronze button, centered: “Continue”

The drawing is original. Do not use a photograph. Do not reuse the landing image, the log in image, or the invitation-on-linen image from the start-a-wedding screen. One drawing only. No second flower, no wreath, no border of flowers around the card, no gold foil, no glitter, no sparkle.

Mobile, 390 wide. The same header: logo left, “Priya · Log out” right. The card uses the width of the screen with comfortable side margins, and sits near the top with space above it. The same order, the same words, the same drawing, the same double hairline. Padding inside the card is about 32px. The button is the full width of the inner card. Text does not overflow. Touch targets are easy to tap.

Do not show a place, a city, a venue, or a map. Do not list events, guests, tasks, or a budget. Do not add a second card, confetti, balloons, hearts, rings, fireworks, emojis, icons, or a photograph. Do not add another sentence, a share button, or a second button. Do not change the colors.
