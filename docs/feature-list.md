# MakeMyMarriage — version one features

The list to approve before a PRD. Major features, then the pieces inside each one.

## 1. Accounts and family

The bride and groom are admins. Family members are helpers. Each person signs up with their own email and is linked to the wedding, the couple, and the family.

- Sign up with email
- Sign in
- Home shows the weddings this person belongs to, with dates
- Admins can do everything in the wedding
- Admins and joined helpers invite family helpers
- Only admins invite the partner as an admin or remove helpers
- A helper is invited by email, signs up with that email, and joins that wedding
- Family record for each person: name, email, and relation to the bride or groom (mother, father, brother, sister, or other)
- One shared wedding workspace

## 2. Wedding and events

- Create the wedding
- Set the wedding dates
- Start from the North Indian Hindu events: roka / engagement, haldi, mehendi, sangeet, wedding ceremony, reception, vidaai
- Admins and family helpers can rename, reorder, add, and remove events
- Guests cannot edit events
- Each event has its own date, time, and venue

## 3. Tasks

- Add a task
- Assign a task to a helper or an admin
- A person can accept a task
- Admins can do all of this
- Helpers can add, assign, and accept tasks
- See tasks for the wedding

## 4. Budget and expenses

Wedding spend only. Guest travel is not tracked.

- Add an expense
- Category (venue, caterer, decor, clothes, ritual, other)
- Amount
- Who paid
- What is still due
- A simple budget total against what is spent
- Admins and family helpers can see and handle this

## 5. Guests and digital invitations

- Admins and family helpers can add a guest (name, email)
- Admins and family helpers can choose which events that guest is invited to
- Admins create the wedding’s digital invitation: the couple, a short message, and the events with dates and venues
- Admins and family helpers can send the digital invitation
- Each guest gets their own invitation, with their name and only the events they are invited to
- The invitation is a card in the product, not a plain text email
- It is sent by email
- The guest opens it in the browser with no account
- The guest RSVPs yes or no for each invited event on the invitation
- An email reminder opens the same invitation
- Admins and family helpers see replies on the guest list, per event
- Guests cannot edit the invitation
- Guests are not contacted on WhatsApp

## 6. Wedding website

A public page for the wedding.

- Schedule of events
- Venue for each event
- RSVP from the guest’s digital invitation
- The embedded YouTube player for an event
- The photo gallery

## 7. Live video (embedded YouTube)

- Admins attach a YouTube video to an event
- The player is embedded, so the video plays inside MakeMyMarriage, in the app and on the wedding website
- YouTube hosts the stream and the recording
- MakeMyMarriage does not run its own streaming

## 8. Photo gallery

One gallery, because different people hold different photos.

- Admins and helpers add photos they have
- The family views the gallery in the app
- Guests view the gallery on the wedding website

## 9. WhatsApp share for organizers

For admins and helpers. Not for guest invites.

- A WhatsApp share button on organizing details: the schedule, an event, and a task
- The button opens WhatsApp with that detail so the family can share it in their own chat

## Out of this version

- Guest travel and stay
- Our own live-streaming servers
- WhatsApp for guest invitation, reminder, or RSVP
- Separate bride-side and groom-side workspaces
- A planner tool for many client weddings
- Wedding types other than North Indian Hindu
- The price families pay to use the app (still open)
